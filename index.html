Ledger Draft
A first-draft assistant for independent CA practices

Give your reviewing partner back the hours the year-end spends on drafting.

Compliance letters, audit observations, and client summaries follow the same shape every time — only the names and numbers change. Ledger Draft turns your firm's details into a ready-to-review draft in minutes, so the partner's time goes to judgment, not typing.

The problem

Even at senior levels, a meaningful share of year-end work isn't judgment — it's writing. The same compliance reminder, the same structure of audit observation, the same client letter, drafted from scratch every time because no two clients' details are identical.

Ledger Draft doesn't replace review. It removes the blank page — so what reaches the partner is already most of the way there, waiting to be checked, not written.

How it works

1

Choose the document

Compliance reminder, audit observation, client summary, or a GST notice reply.

2

Fill in the specifics

Client name, the facts of the matter, the figures involved — and optionally attach a supporting document.

3

Generate the draft

Ledger Draft sends your details securely and returns a finished first draft, right on the page.

4

Copy it out

Copy the draft into your firm's usual document, email, or template.

5

Review before it leaves the firm

Every draft is exactly that — a draft. The partner's review is still what makes it final.

Build a draft

Details you enter are sent securely to generate the draft — never stored, and never used for anything beyond producing this one document.

Generated draft

This produces a first draft only. Figures, section references, and enclosures should always be verified by the firm before a document is sent to a client or department.

Ledger Draft is a free tool for drafting first-pass documents. It does not store, transmit, or retain anything you enter.
